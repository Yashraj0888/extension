## 1. What AfterMeet is

AfterMeet is a **local-first** tool that:

- Captures caption text from Google Meet in your browser
- Stores transcripts and related notes in your browser / on your machine
- Optionally sends transcript text to **AI providers you configure** (for summaries, chat, action items, and similar features)
- Optionally connects to Google Calendar using credentials **you** provide

AfterMeet is not a hosted SaaS product. Maintainers of this repository do **not** operate a backend that stores your meeting content.
